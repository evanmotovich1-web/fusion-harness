Ensuring a Healthier, More Equitable Future!
Welcome to the Social Health Authority, committed to managing and facilitating funding to ensure the delivery of high-quality healthcare services through accredited facilities and trusted providers.
Learn More
Why SHA?
SHA ensures that all citizens have access to quality healthcare without restrictions. With no age limits, no exclusions for pre-existing conditions, and unlimited dependent registration, SHA provides comprehensive and inclusive coverage for all.
No Maximum Age Limit
Nobody is too old to register and enjoy benefits provided by Social Health Authority
No Pre-existing Conditions
SHA doesn't check for pre-existing health conditions.
No Maximum Number of Children
SHA does not have a limit on the number of legal children one can add.

Premium Rates
The Social Health Authority (SHA) operates a two-tier contribution system to fund the Social Health Insurance Fund (SHIF)—one of three national health insurance funds (PHF, SHIF, ECCIF) under its management.
Learn More
Means Testing
Means testing is a fair and scientific way of figuring out how much someone can afford to pay for social health insurance. It’s used around the world and helps make sure that people contribute based on their actual income and living situation.
Learn More
LIPA POLE POLE
LIPA SHA POLE POLE is a simple, flexible payment plan that helps Kenyans in the informal sector pay for their Social Health Insurance in small, manageable instalments. This means you can access essential healthcare without the stress of paying a large lump sum upfront.
Learn More
Benefit Tariffs
The Social Health Authority (SHA) has established standardized tariffs and benefit packages to ensure transparency, equity, and affordability in the delivery of healthcare services under the Social Health Insurance Act No. 16 of 2023.

Eligibility for SHA Registration
All residents of Kenya, including Kenyan citizens, refugees, and other non-citizens, are eligible to register for SHA.
Kenyan Citizens
National Identification Card (ID)
Children
Birth certificate
Foreign Residents
Foreign Resident Certificate (Alien ID) or Refugee ID (for refugees)
Register Today!
Adding Dependants
You can list your dependents (spouse and children) during your initial registration. There is no limit to the number of children you can register.
To add a spouse later, you will need to submit a marriage certificate or sworn affidavit with a copy of their ID.
To add a child born after registration, notify SHA within 14 days of birth and provide a birth notification or certificate.
