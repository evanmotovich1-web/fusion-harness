Understanding the Three Health Funds under SHA

The Social Health Authority (SHA) is making healthcare more accessible and affordable for all Kenyans. Under this system, there are three different health funds, each designed to meet specific healthcare needs.

Here’s a simple breakdown of what each fund does, what’s covered, and how you can benefit.

Primary Healthcare Fund Benefit Tariffs

What the Primary Healthcare Fund (PHF) covers:

This fund takes care of everyday health needs. It’s focused on services you get at your local clinic or health centre — especially Levels 2, 3, and some Level 4 facilities.

Outpatient Care Services
ScopeAccess PointTariffAccess Rules
  1. Consultation, diagnosis, and treatment
  2. Prescribed laboratory investigations
  3. Basic radiological examinations including X- rays, ultrasounds.
  4. Prescription processing, drug administration and dispensing.
  5. Health education and wellness, counselling, and ongoing support as needed.
  6. Management of acute and chronic conditions for endemic/local cases
  7. Management of non-communicable diseases (NCDs), enteric infections; neglected tropical diseases (NTDs), sexually transmitted infections (STI’s) and HIV/AIDS
  8. Mental Health education, counselling and psychosocial support services.
  9. Screening for common health conditions.
  10. Minor outpatient procedures in the procedure room or under local anaesthesia.
  11. Immunization as per the Kenya Expanded Program on Immunization (KEPI) schedule and recommended special vaccines.
  12. Reproductive, Maternal (including antenatal and postnatal care services) and Child Health Services as defined by the Ministry of Health guidelines.
Level 2, 3 and select Level 4 primary healthcare referral facilities as contracted by the Authority to offer primary healthcare services.KES. 900 per person per annum
  1. All registered beneficiaries will be mapped to a Primary Care Network (PCN) at the point of registration, where they can access services in any facility within their Primary Care Network.
  2. The global budget capitation shall be allocated based on the population in the Primary Care Network (PCN).
  3. Distribution of the Funds shall be done at the end of the month based on patient visits, weighted by disease treated.

  3. Treatment by chiropractors, acupuncturists, and herbalists,
  4. Assisted fertility treatment by In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) or any other procedure.

  8. Weight management treatment drugs.
  9. Nutritional supplements.
  10. Shampoos (except where certified as a necessary part of treatment).
  11. Medical costs related to or incurred in a research environment and clinical trials.
  12. Travel expenses other than ambulance costs as defined under emergency care and referral services.
  13. Stop-smoking aids.
  14. Epidemics and pandemics.
  15. Hormone replacement therapy (HRT), unless in connection with, and immediately after a pre-authorized surgical procedure or unless otherwise provided for under the terms and conditions of the treatment plan.
  16. Admission in a private ward, stay at a sanatorium or old age home, treatment in a spa, sauna, health farm, or similar establishment, stays and/or maintenance or treatment received in nature cure clinics or similar establishment or, convalescent and or rest homes or cures attached to such establishments.

  18. Expenses related to diagnostic equipment including but not limited to such as glucometer or glucose machine.

Emergency, Chronic & Critical Illness Fund Benefit Tariffs

What Emergency, Chronic & Critical Illness Fund (ECCIF) covers:

This fund covers life-threatening emergencies, chronic illnesses, and critical care. It’s your safety net when immediate or long-term serious care is needed.

Ambulance Evacuation Services
ScopeAccess PointTariffAccess Rules
Evacuation from the point of the incident to the nearest facility with an Accident and Emergency center and the transfer of patients requiring emergency and further clinical care from the facility.Management of all transfers/evacuations through National & County Ambulance Call Centres.
  1. 25 km Radius (Intra metro) – KES. 4,500
  2. > 25 km (Extra metro) – KES. 75 per km
Accessible by all Kenyans irrespective of registration or premium payment status.

ScopeAccess PointTariffAccess Rules

This cover includes resuscitation and stabilization due to:

  1. Cardiac and pulmonary arrest
  2. Major trauma (severe burns, head injuries, severe wounds, multiple fractures)

  4. Altered level of consciousness (unconsciousness, confusion) Severe respiratory distress
  5. Seizures.
  6. Chest pain (Acute Coronary Syndrome/ Pulmonary embolism) Acute Cerebrovascular Accidents (Strokes).
  7. Anti Snake Venom & Anti Rabies.
