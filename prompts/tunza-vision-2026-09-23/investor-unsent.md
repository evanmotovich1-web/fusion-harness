## The job

Tunza is being built to help someone decide what happens next when a person is sick and care is far away, rather than name a diagnosis. [1]

## Proposed use of a check

These are proposals for discussion, not Evan's commitments or work already secured. [2]

- **P1 — County introduction.** Seek an introduction in one county.
- **P2 — Data rights.** Prepare a page setting out the data rights that would be needed.
- **P3 — Shadow period.** Propose a period alongside normal care where patients are not the test and the demo does not direct their care.

This proposal is not funding a trained model or a national rollout. [2]

## What exists now

The public demo has household, community health worker, and facility views, with English and Kiswahili copy. Its demonstration rules produce a next-step category: go now, get care today, monitor at home, or ask for one more answer. [3]

Cases stay in the browser. The referral path uses fake demo facilities. It is not evidence of a working connection to a receiving clinic. [4]

The rules are not clinically validated. This is not a substitute for emergency services or professional medical care. [5]

## Strategy questions still unanswered

- **Q1 — Label.** What outcome belongs in the label column, who records it, and when?
- **Q2 — Data rights.** What permission would allow the intended data use, including any training?
- **Q3 — One product or two.** Are we building case prioritization for eCHIS, a household question service, or two separate products?
- **Q4 — Precision floor.** How many false urgent alerts can a community health worker tolerate before they stop opening the app?

These remain questions for Evan, not decisions made by this draft. [6]
