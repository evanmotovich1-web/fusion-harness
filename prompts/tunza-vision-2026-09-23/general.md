This is for someone who wants the company in one sitting.

## The job

Someone is sick, care is far, and the question is what should happen next, not what the diagnosis is.

## Who it is for

A household. A community health worker. The facility that receives the person.

In this demo those roles are household, CHP, and facility. A household can start with no worker grant. CHP and facility sit behind a gate. That gate is a placeholder for real identity. Until real codes are set, it runs in demo mode.

## What a person can do in this demo

The words on screen are in English and Kiswahili.

A person can check symptoms, look at nearby facilities, or continue what is already on that device.

Check symptoms takes a short account in text. Voice is one tap to speak. If the server speech path is not set up, the demo tries speech on the device, then asks the person to type. A photo can be marked attached. The screen says "Photo attached (demo)". The rules do not look at the picture.

The demo asks who this is, what is happening, whether they are awake, whether they are breathing, whether they are drinking, how long it has been, and what the main problem is.

The result is one of four next steps: go now, get care today, monitor at home, or need one more answer. Each result carries the same warning signs. The rules listen for a few danger words in English and Kiswahili.

Nearby facilities can look up places when that lookup is configured. If it is not, the screen says so and shows demo facilities. A name and a distance are not proof the place can do what the person needs.

The demo can walk a referral from created through outcome returned. The facility on that path is a fake demo facility, not a live place.

The case stays in the browser on that device. It is not a shared record.

## What it is not

It is not a doctor. It is not a diagnosis. The rules are a demonstration. They are deliberately conservative. They are not clinically validated.

The screen says: "Not a substitute for emergency services or professional medical care."

It does not read a photo. It does not keep cases on a server. It does not choose a real facility that has confirmed it can take the person.

## Where it is trying to go

Intention only. We want a referral followed far enough to learn whether the next step was right. That is designed. It is not built.
