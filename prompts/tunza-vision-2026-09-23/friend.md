## What the demo lets a person do

This is the public demo. A person can open it and walk a case on that device.

The words on screen are in English and Kiswahili.

A person can start as a household, with no worker grant. The other roles in the demo are a CHP and a facility. CHP is the community health worker role in this demo. If someone picks CHP or facility without a grant, the demo sends them back to the household door and opens a gate. That gate is a placeholder for real identity. It is demo mode until real codes are set. This note does not print those codes.

On the home screen the person can check symptoms, look at nearby facilities, or continue. The check-symptoms card says text, voice, or photo.

Text is a short account of what is happening.

Voice is one tap to speak. If the server speech path is not set up, the demo tries speech on the device, then asks the person to type.

A photo can be marked as attached. The screen says "Photo attached (demo)". The rules do not look at the picture.

The demo then asks, in this order: who this is, what is happening, whether they are awake, whether they are breathing, whether they are drinking, how long it has been, and what the main problem is.

The result is one of four next steps: go now, get care today, monitor at home, or need one more answer. Each result carries the same warning signs. The rules listen for a few danger words in English and Kiswahili. They are a demonstration. They are deliberately conservative. They are not clinically validated.

The screen says: "Not a substitute for emergency services or professional medical care."

The demo can move a referral through these stages: created, sent, received, accepted, patient moving, arrived, seen, completed, and outcome returned. The facility on that referral is a fake demo facility. It is not chosen from a live place search.

Nearby facilities is a separate screen. When lookup is configured, the demo can ask for places near a coarse area. If lookup is not available, the screen says so and shows demo facilities. A nearby name and a distance are not proof the place can do what the person needs.

The case stays in that browser. It is not a server record. Continue on the home screen is not a history of past cases. That history is not built.

The demo can also store a local outcome: treated, referred onward, did not arrive, or unknown. That is a mark in the browser. It is not something the demo learned.

## What it refuses to be

It is not a doctor. It does not give a diagnosis.

The rules are a demonstration. They are not clinically validated.

It does not read a photo. It does not send the case to a server. It does not pick a live facility for the referral.

This note is about the public demo only. It is not a description of an older private tool.

He wants a later version to follow a referral far enough to learn whether the next step was right. That is a want. The demo does not do it.
