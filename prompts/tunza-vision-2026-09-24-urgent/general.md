This is for someone who wants the company in one sitting.

## The stake

eCHIS does not order which sick child goes first. The care path can then fail at the decision, destination, facility, travel, acknowledgement, or returned-result step. Tunza has to exist as an attempt to solve that gap. The demo does not do this yet.

Whether that wait kills a child is unknown; no checked source documents that death. What is documented is the country's toll: the WHO Kenya 2025 report puts maternal mortality at an estimated 355 deaths per 100,000 live births, from preventable causes, and Kenya's Ministry of Health ties a fragmented referral system to maternal and child health outcomes in the referral policy it is developing. Those are the country's numbers, not Tunza's results.

The Ministry of Health is developing a national referral policy on continuity, interoperability, and accountability. The Ministry ties that work to maternal and child health outcomes. That is public context in a discussion brief. It is not evidence that Tunza implements the policy, and it is not a partnership. Reference only, not an endorsement: https://health.go.ke/kenya-moves-strengthen-patient-referral-system-through-new-national-policy

## Who it is for

A household. A community health worker, the CHP in this demo. The facility that receives the person.

A household can start with no worker grant. CHP and facility sit behind a placeholder gate that runs in demo mode until real identity exists.

## What a person can do in this demo

The words on screen are English and Kiswahili. A person can check symptoms, look at nearby facilities, or continue what is already on that device.

The check takes a short account in text. Voice is one tap to speak, then type if speech is not available. A photo can be marked attached. The rules do not look at the picture.

The result is one of four next steps: go now, get care today, monitor at home, or need one more answer.

## What it is not

It is not a doctor. It is not a diagnosis. The rules are a demonstration. They are not clinically validated. The screen says this is not a substitute for emergency services or professional medical care.

The case stays on that device. It is not a shared record. The referral uses a fake demo facility, not a live place that has confirmed it can take the person.

## Where it is trying to go

Intention only. We want a referral followed far enough to learn whether the next step was right. That is designed. It is not built. Ordering cases and this question-and-next-step demo are not the same shipped thing.
