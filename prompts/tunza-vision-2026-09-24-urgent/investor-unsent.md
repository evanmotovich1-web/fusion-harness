# Tunza — investor draft

## The stake

eCHIS does not order which sick child goes first. The care path can then fail at the decision, destination, facility, travel, acknowledgement, or returned-result step. Tunza has to exist as an attempt to solve that gap. The demo does not do this yet. [1]

Those failures are different costs of the same interval: a clinical decision that was wrong; a referral to the wrong place; a facility that could not provide the service; a patient who could not travel; a referral never acknowledged; a result that never came back to the community. [2]

A score in a database changes nothing until a named person acts. [3]

Whether that wait kills a child is unknown; no checked source documents that death. What is documented is the country's toll: WHO Kenya 2025, printed page 60, estimates 355 maternal deaths per 100,000 live births, from preventable causes including postpartum haemorrhage and hypertensive disorders. Kenya's Ministry of Health names referral fragmentation and ties its developing referral policy to maternal and child health outcomes. Those are the country's numbers. They are not Tunza's results, not a market-size claim, and this page still has no lives-saved number. [4][11][12]

Kenya’s Ministry of Health is developing a national referral policy on continuity, interoperability, and accountability. The country already supports more than 107,000 Community Health Promoters, per the Ministry of Health — and none of that scale decides which sick child moves first. [13] That is public context, not a partnership, not an endorsement, and not evidence that Tunza implements the policy. The first decision is still unordered. Reference only: https://health.go.ke/kenya-moves-strengthen-patient-referral-system-through-new-national-policy [5]

## What exists now

The public demo has household, community health worker, and facility views, with English and Kiswahili copy. Its demonstration rules produce a next-step category: go now, get care today, monitor at home, or ask for one more answer. [7]

It is not a doctor. It is not a diagnosis. The rules are not clinically validated. Cases stay in the browser. The referral path uses fake demo facilities. It is not evidence of a working connection to a receiving clinic. It is not a substitute for emergency services or professional medical care. [8]

The public question-and-next-step demo is not the eCHIS ordering product. That ordering is designed. It is not built. [9]
