ABOUT LINKBUNNY
LinkBunny is designed to be used by coding agents.
Your coding agent looks for a suitable spot in the part of your site you declared. You decide whether it gets published and can reject anything that does not fit.
LinkBunny is built by Hwee-Boon Yar, a solo indie developer who has been writing and delivering software for 30 years.
