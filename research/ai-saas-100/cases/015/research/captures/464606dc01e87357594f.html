Pricing FAQs

How does Anyword work?

Anyword has a simple and flexible pricing system that allows you to choose a plan based on your business needs and goals. You can opt for a monthly or annual subscription plan that best suits your budget.

What's included in Anyword's plans?

Our plans include access to our Data-Driven Copy Generation Suite, the Blog Wizard, Unlimited Words, Brand Voice & Copy Intelligence features (like Predictive Scoring and Analytics), expert support, and regular updates to improve your performance writing experience.

How many users can join my plan?

Starter plans include 1 seat. Data-Driven plan starts with 3 seats, and you can add up to 10 seats. Business plan includes 3 seats and you can add up to 20 seats. Our enterprise plan is custom-built for your organization’s needs.

Can I change my subscription plan at any time?

Yes, you can update your subscription plan at any time. Simply log in to your account, select the plan you want, and the system will automatically adjust your subscription fees.

What does unlimited words mean?

Every Anyword paid plan comes with Unlimited Words. This means that no matter how many copy variations you generate in a month, you will not be limited by the number of words you use.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes, we offer a 7-day free trial with a limit of 2,500 words. You can sign up for a free trial to experience the power of our Gen AI Performance Writing and then decide which plan is right for you..

Can I cancel my subscription at anytime?

Yes, you can cancel monthly or yearly subscriptions at any time. However, please note that we don’t offer refunds for canceled subscriptions.

Are there discounts for non-profit organizations or students?

While we do not have any dedicated discounts for non-profits at this time, we run promotions regularly. Be sure to check the emails we send you and follow Anyword on social media to stay informed about new discounts, features, guides, playbooks, and more!

What if I have questions or issues with my subscription?

We have a dedicated support team ready to assist you with any questions or issues you may encounter with your subscription. You can reach out to us through our contact page or email info@anyword.com, and we’ll respond as soon as possible.

What are performance data rows?

Performance data rows are essential for improving the precision of Anyword’s content generation and prediction tools. Each row contains performance metrics gathered through integrations, and these metrics are updated daily as long as the integrations remain active. The number of rows available depends on your chosen plan, with higher tiers offering more rows for deeper insights and optimization.

What are performance predictions?

Performance predictions allow you to see how effective your copy will be before it goes live. These predictions evaluate your content variations based on their potential to convert or engage your audience. The number of predictions available depends on your chosen plan, with higher tiers offering more performance predictions for deeper insights and optimization.
