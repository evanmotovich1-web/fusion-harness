Integration questions, answered straight.
Are integrations included on every Frase plan?
Yes. Every plan, starting at $39/mo billed yearly, includes CMS publishing, Google Search Console, imports, exports, Slack notifications, and API access. There are no separate per-integration fees. Frase does sell extra seats, audit credits, and AI prompts as your usage grows, and yearly billing saves 20%.
Which CMS platforms can Frase publish to?
Frase publishes directly to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, and Wix, or hosts your content on FraseCMS. Connect your CMS once and Frase handles formatting, metadata, featured images, and SEO fields when you click publish — as a draft, scheduled, or live — without opening your CMS dashboard.
Do I need to install a plugin to publish to WordPress?
No. Frase publishes to WordPress directly over its API once you connect your site, so an article goes live without a plugin install. Frase also fills your Yoast or Rank Math meta fields and uploads featured images to your Media Library in the process.
Can I import content I've already written?
Yes. Frase imports from Google Docs, Notion, GitHub, Linear, and any URL. Search your workspace, pick a repository, or paste a link — Frase extracts the content, preserves the formatting, and makes it ready to audit and optimize right away.
Does the API work with automation tools like Zapier?
Yes. The REST API supports webhooks, so it works with Zapier, Make, n8n, and any platform that can call a REST endpoint. Build flows that create briefs, trigger audits, and export optimized content automatically.
What if my CMS isn't on the list?
Export any piece as HTML, Markdown, PDF, or plain text for a platform that isn't directly supported, or use the REST API to build a custom publishing flow into your own system.
