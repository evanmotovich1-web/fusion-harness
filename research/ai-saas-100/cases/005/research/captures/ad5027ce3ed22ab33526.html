Questions, answered straight.

What do I get in the 7-day free trial?

Professional's full feature set, including the part most tools make you pay to reach: you can let the agent publish on its own during the trial. Turn it on for a content type, watch what it ships, and turn it off again if you don't like what you see. You also get the content calendar, internal linking, Frase's AI engines for content (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini), and AI Visibility on ChatGPT and Google AI. Quantities are capped at 5 articles, 50 audit pages, 25 AI prompts and up to 3 seats. No credit card to start.

What happens when I hit a monthly limit?

On Starter, Frase holds at the limit until your cycle resets or you upgrade, so a surprise charge never happens. On Professional and Scale, you can switch on pay-as-you-go capacity and keep going. Professional adds $5 per extra article, $0.50 per audit page, and $0.25 per AI prompt. Scale adds $4, $0.40, and $0.20. Overages only apply if you turn them on, and they bill at the end of your cycle.

How do add-ons work?

Extra seats run $29/mo each on Professional and Scale. Audit pages and AI prompts come as pay-as-you-go capacity on those same plans. FraseCMS hosting is an add-on too: free with any plan, then $19/mo for Basic or $99/mo for Pro on top of it. Add what you need, when you need it. The sticker price is your floor, not a number that creeps up behind your back.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade whenever you want and the change is prorated on the spot. Downgrade and it takes hold at your next billing cycle. Choose yearly billing and 20% comes off the monthly rate.

What does Content Guard do, and what stays in my control?

When a published page starts losing Google rank or traffic, Content Guard writes the fix and republishes it to your CMS once you approve. Approval is the default on every change. You can opt in to let Frase ship low-risk fixes on their own, and that switch is always yours to flip back. AI Visibility is a separate feature: it monitors AI engines and alerts you, and it never touches your content.

Which CMS platforms does Frase publish to?

Frase publishes to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, and Wix, or hosts the content for you on FraseCMS — an agent-ready CMS, hosted or headless, with WebMCP so AI assistants can read your pages and act on them.

How much does FraseCMS cost?

The free tier is included with any Frase plan: 100,000 page views a month and 25 GB of bandwidth. Basic and Pro are add-ons on top of your plan. Basic is $19/mo for 250,000 page views, a subdirectory of your own site if you want one, and no Frase badge. Pro is $99/mo for a million page views. Yearly billing saves two months on either. Past the cap, the free tier pauses until the month resets; Basic and Pro keep serving at $1.00 and $0.50 per extra 1,000 page views, with a spend cap set at your base price by default, so overage cannot silently run away. A page view counts each time a page is served to a reader or pulled through the API.

Is Scale enough for my agency, or do I need Enterprise?

For most agencies, Scale is plenty: up to 10 domains, 5 seats with roles, client-ready reports you can export, all self-serve. Turning one piece of content into posts for every channel starts on Professional. The line is white-label reports or a branded client portal. The moment you need one of those, that is Enterprise. Talk to sales and we will set it up.

What can I realistically expect Frase to do?

Frase produces real, optimized content, surfaces the gaps in the pages you already have, and moves your in-app SEO, GEO, and AI-visibility scores as you apply the fixes. Rankings and AI citations are the slow part. They build over the weeks after you publish, so Frase shows you the work going out and tracks the signals coming back. What Frase will not do is promise you a ranking or a citation inside a 7-day window.
