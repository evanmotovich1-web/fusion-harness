Do I need to build an integration before I can be listed on the Marketplace?

Yes. To be listed on the Instantly Marketplace, you must have a working integration that meets our technical requirements and demonstrates real customer usage or clear activation potential.

‍

Step 1: Get Started

The first step is to complete the Get Started form so we can route your integration through the proper review and launch flow and set you up for success:

‍

Step 2: Submit Your Application (after completion only)

Once your integration is complete, you will be invited to submit your official application. Only submit this form after the integration is finished:

‍

Even if your integration is already complete, it’s important to follow this process to ensure your listing is reviewed and published accurately. After completing the Get Started form and required steps, you’ll receive an email with instructions to submit your application.

Are there any fees to list my integration in the Instantly Marketplace?

No. There are no fees to list your integration.

Our goal is to highlight high-quality integrations that help customers get better results with Instantly and with your product.

Do you offer an affiliate or referral program for integration partners?

Yes — if your integration drives new Instantly customers, you can participate in our PartnerStack Integration Partner Program. This allows you to track referred accounts, measure impact, and unlock additional co-marketing opportunities.

We strongly recommend setting this up, as partners with referral tracking enabled are prioritized for:

  • Marketplace placement and visibility
  • Expanded co-marketing opportunities
  • Campaigns and partner spotlights to our audience and user base
Commission options
  • 0% commission – For partners who want referral tracking only and prefer to remain impartial.
  • All integration partners are assigned this by default.
  • Up to 20% lifetime commission – Available for qualified partners where there is a strong mutual fit.

Note: All Integration Partners start at 0% commission. If you’re interested in earning commissions, simply create your PartnerStack account and send a message directly within the PartnerStack platform to request an upgrade.
