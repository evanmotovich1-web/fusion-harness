What is an AI avatar generator?

An AI avatar generator is a tool that creates digital presenters using artificial intelligence. It can turn a script into a video of a realistic or animated avatar speaking the text, with generated voice, lip sync, facial expressions, and body movements. This lets you create presenter-led videos without filming a real person.

Language coverage: 177+ languages and dialects. Most tools do one or two of these well. HeyGen does all four, which is why it holds a 4.8 on G2 across 1,000+ reviews.

How do I create an AI avatar of myself?

Two ways. Record about 30 seconds to camera and HeyGen builds a custom avatar with your face, expressions, and delivery. Or upload one front-facing photo for a faster result. No design or editing skills needed. If you publish weekly, bulk generation turns a spreadsheet of scripts into finished videos in one run.
