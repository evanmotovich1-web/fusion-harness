Data Security
You Can Trust

Security isn’t just a feature for us—it’s at the heart of everything we do. At Paperpal, your research and data are protected by robust encryption, privacy-first measures, and a policy of never using your data to train its AI. Our secure-by-design ecosystem lets you focus on your writing, while we keep your information safe.

Confidentiality Agreements

Responsible AI you can rely on 

Transparent and Secure AI Usage 

Paperpal uses advanced AI models—including proprietary and fine-tuned systems—tailored for scholarly writing. Whether you are a Prime member or a free Paperpal user, your data will never be used to train our AI models. With robust guardrails in place to mitigate incorrect or malicious use, our AI provides reliable assistance without security risks.

Trusted Infrastructure. Total Protection.

Paperpal’s enterprise-grade security measures protect your data at every step.

Certified Data Centers

Your data resides safely in SSAE 18/SOC1-certified facilities that are trusted globally.

End-to-End Encryption

Data is encrypted both in transit (256-bit encryption) and at rest (secured with KMS keys).

Rigorous Access Controls

Unauthorized access is blocked with our robust firewalls and strict security protocols.

Our Regulatory Compliance & Security Frameworks

GDPR Compliant

Paperpal operates in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), ensuring responsible handling of personal data.

PECR Ready

Paperpal adheres to the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), also known as the e-Privacy Directive, supporting user privacy in digital communications.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certified

Paperpal's Information Security Management System (ISMS) is certified to the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 international standard, reflecting our commitment to rigorous security protocols.

CSA STAR - Level 1 Listed

Paperpal is listed at Level 1 in the Cloud Security Alliance’s Security, Trust, Assurance, and Risk (STAR) Registry, demonstrating adherence to industry-recognized best practices.

FERPA Compliant

Paperpal aligns with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), protecting student’s personal data and education records at institutions receiving US Department of Education funding.

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Certified

Paperpal’s Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) is certified to the ISO/IEC 42001:2023 international standard, demonstrating our commitment to responsible, ethical, and well-governed use of AI.

Detection, Response, Recovery: End-to-End Risk Management

Application Testing

We conduct mandatory security assessments for every platform update. Automated scanners and third-party Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) certifications ensure vulnerabilities are identified and addressed.

Security Event Response Plan

All security events are analyzed and resolved by our emergency engineering team, followed by a retrospective to prevent future occurrences.

Disaster Recovery

With backups across multiple regions, your manuscripts remain accessible even in the rarest of disruptions.

Build Process Automation

Security patches and platform updates are deployed seamlessly throughout the day, keeping you protected without interruptions.
