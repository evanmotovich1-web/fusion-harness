FAQs

Is it free?

Absolutely! We're introducing our basic plan with zero charges, ensuring that our tool is within everyone's reach. The basic plan delivers a resume builder, unlimited resume downloads, an overall resume score, 1 resume upload, 5 bullet points generated by ResumAI, 1 cover letter created with CoverLetterAI, and 1 networking message crafted by NetworkAI.

If you're a fan of our tool, upgrading to the premium plan unlocks unlimited access to ResumAI, CoverLetterAI, and NetworkAI. You'll also get an in-depth resume score breakdown, full access to InterviewAI, JobTrackerAI and the Learning Hub.

What's included in the paid subscription plan?

Simply put, it includes everything! With the premium subscription, you unlock full access to ResumAI, InterviewAI, CoverLetterAI, and NetworkAI features. This grants you the freedom to upload unlimited resumes and generate an infinite number of resume bullet points, cover letters, and networking messages.

Moreover, the package encompasses our Resume Scorer, which exclusively delivers a comprehensive resume score breakdown to premium users, analyzing your resume's quality.

How does subscription to WonsultingAI work?

For just $19.99 a month, you'll enjoy unrestricted access to our array of AI-powered products through our premium monthly subscription plan. Your payments will recur on a monthly basis until you opt to cancel.

Who can benefit from WonsultingAI?

This service is tailored to individuals who are in need of professional-level guidance during their job search, and it comes at a budget-friendly price!

Will the subscription price ever increase in the future?

While the future is unpredictable, we value transparency. By subscribing to our product today at the current price, you’ll be locked in. Your subscription cost will remain the same as long as you stay subscribed, regardless of future price changes. It’s our way of thanking you for your early support and loyalty!

What are the payment methods supported by WonsultingAI?

You can make payments effortlessly using credit or debit cards.

Can I only subscribe to one feature (ResumAI / CoverLetterAI / NetworkAI / InterviewAI) and not the rest?

Presently, we don't have the feature-specific subscription option. Our focus is on making sure that all users experience the full benefits of our features, giving your job applications a powerful edge.

How can I delete my WonsultingAI account?

To remove your WonsultingAI Account, follow these simple steps:
1. Click on "Settings" located in the lower left corner of the website.
2. Proceed to the "Account" Tab.
3. Choose the "Delete Account" option.
