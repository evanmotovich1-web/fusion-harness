What are the various pricing plans that Botsonic offers?

Botsonic offers four main pricing plans to suit different business needs:

1. Starter Plan: Ideal for beginners, priced at $16 per month (billed annually) or $19 monthly. It includes 1 chatbot, 1,000 messages per month, and basic features to get you started.
‍
2. Professional Plan: Designed for growing businesses at $40.83 per month (billed annually) or $49 monthly. It offers 2 chatbots, 3,000 messages per month, and additional integrations.

3. Advanced Plan: For businesses looking to scale, priced at $249 per month (billed annually) or $299 monthly. It provides 12,000 messages per month, AI Agentic Workflows for automated tasks, and advanced features.
‍
4. Enterprise Plan: Tailored for large organizations with custom pricing. It offers the highest level of customization, advanced AI capabilities, and priority support.

All plans come with a 7-day free trial. Each plan is designed to grow with your business, offering flexibility in terms of messages, chatbots, and features.

Additional add-ons are available for extra messages, uploaded characters, team members, and integrations to further customize your plan.

How can Botsonic's AI Agentic actions improve my business?

AI Agents can dramatically improve business operations in several key areas:

‍Personalized Customer Interaction: AI Agents trained on your data can interact with customers 24/7, providing tailored support and enhancing the overall customer experience.

‍Automated Employee Assistance: They streamline internal processes, offering employees instant access to information which improves efficiency and workplace morale.

‍Conversational Commerce: These agents engage customers in real-time, providing personalized shopping advice and support, helping drive sales and customer satisfaction.

‍Advanced AI Workflows: Moving beyond scripted responses, AI Agents can intelligently automate customer experiences, making autonomous decisions that adapt to the conversation in real-time.

AI Agents serve as versatile tools that can improve customer and employee satisfaction, boost sales, and optimize operational efficiency.

What addon options are available?

We offer several addon options to customize and expand your Botsonic experience:

1. Additional Chatbots: You can add up to 3 extra bots for $99 per month.

2. Extra Team Members: Add team members at $25 per month each, with the ability to expand up to 10 members.

3. Botsonic Branding Removal: Remove Botsonic branding for a monthly fee of $49.

4. Additional Character Allowances: For more data uploads, purchase additional character allowances at $10 for every additional 20M characters per month.

5. Additional Messages: The Professional Plan comes with 3,000 messages/month by default. Need more?

Here are your options:
2,000 extra messages/month: $25/month
4,000 extra messages/month: $50/month
8,000 extra messages/month: $100/month
16,000 extra messages/month: $200/month

6. Support Handoff Integrations: Botsonic can handle up to 70% of your customer queries. For the remaining 30%, you can do a live agent or ticket handoff to your Zendesk or Freshdesk helpdesk.

Are there any discounts available on Botsonic pricing plans?

Yes, we offer multiple discount options to provide you with the best value:
‍
Annual Discount: Subscribe to our annual plans and get 2 months free. That means you effectively get 12 months of service for the cost of 10 months.

Educational and Non-Profit Discount: We offer a special 30% discount for eligible educational institutions and non-profit organizations. Please contact our support team to avail of this offer.

I have exhausted my messages before the start of the next billing period. How can I get more?

If you've used up your allotted messages before the next billing period, you can purchase additional messages as needed. Botsonic will only charge the pro-rated amount based on the number of days remaining in your current billing cycle. If your messaging needs decrease the following month, you can remove the addons and your charges will be adjusted accordingly.

Is there any pay-as-you-go plan?

Currently, we don't offer a pay-as-you-go plan. Only monthly and annual plans are available.

If I subscribe to a monthly plan, will I be charged automatically next month?

Yes, monthly subscription plans charge you automatically every month until you cancel or pause them.

What are the payment methods supported by Botsonic?

We currently accept debit/credit cards as well as PayPal as payment methods.

On the Enterprise plan, invoicing options are available as well that allow payments via ACH and wire transfers if applicable.
