Meet the team

The Short Story

Trevor Labs, ltd. is aiming to solve some of the fundamental challenges of time management, in modern-day life, with an artificially intelligent scheduling assistant. The company is based in Europe and was started with the support of the Founder Institute. Trevor Labs was founded in February 2016, by George Petrov, with its Technical Co-founder Dmitry Yudakov joining soon after. Ever since the co-founders have committed more than 44896 lines of code. The first problem Trevor AI solves is the disconnect between time and task management. With a unique drag & drop interface and some backend magic, app.trevorai.com was launched - a web app, available on mobile and desktop that integrates your task list with your Google calendar to help you schedule your tasks. Realistic planning just got a lot easier and scheduling your whole day takes seconds. After aggregating a good amount of data, Trevor Labs also launched a Messenger chat bot that automagically helps you find time for your tasks in a very natural way.

Special thanks to our early partners and advisors: Todor Kolev, Ivan Vasilev, Miro Z. and Daniella Ilieva, PhD.

What’s Next

At Trevor Labs, we believe that every modern “hero” deserves an AI productivity sidekick - an intelligent software that can recreate the way we naturally manage time in our heads and show us ways we can improve every day.

  • It can offload some of the redundant organizational processes, such as categorization & bundling of tasks,
  • automate rescheduling and scheduling conflicts,
  • reduce scheduling decision fatigue with smart suggestions,
  • propose schedule optimizations based on behavioral patters and success rates,
  • help us visualize and make sense of time analytics data collected over time, just to name a few.

To validate this hypothesis, the company is focusing on teaching Trevor AI to make sense of the aggregated data so far. Calendar data is incredibly sensitive and we take user privacy very seriously. That being said, Trevor can learn patterns that ultimately help make the user’s schedule more efficient and effective.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Trevor AI's capabilities?

Trevor AI is an all-in-one AI planner for your tasks and calendars. Explore all of Trevor AI's capabilities and limitations in our documentation.

Is Trevor AI a Calendar or a To-do List?

Trevor AI is a smart planner that integrates both to-do lists and calendars, giving you the best of both worlds. What makes Trevor AI better than either one is the connection it facilitates between tasks and calendar events, as well as all the AI planning features.

How does Trevor AI plan my daily tasks?

Trevor AI offers an intuitive visual planner for your tasks, meetings and events. You can plan your day with AI in 1 click, you can drag & drop your tasks yourself, or you can simply ask Trevor to do it in the chat. Plan your day, your way.

Is Trevor AI Free?

Yes, a lot of Trevor AI’s features are free for personal use, including some AI planning features. Pro and Executive plans are available for those looking for an extra edge (that pays for itself many times over).

What does the “AI” in Trevor AI do?

Trevor's AI helps with organizing, planning your tasks tasks—From predicting a task's duration, category and even priority, to suggesting it’s optimal time-slot in your busy schedule, to finally breaking it down for you into 5 manageable steps - Trevor AI is with you all the way.

How does Trevor AI compare to Reclaim?

Compared to Trevor AI, Reclaim seems to have an algorithm that uses strict rules for each task. It works great for habits, but if you have over 25 tasks in your list, it can quickly become overwhelming to set rules for each one. While Trevor’s one-click AI scheduling feature is a direct competitor, you can just as easily drag & drop tasks between your task list and your calendar as much as you want, giving you effortless control over your schedule.

How does Trevor AI compare to Motion?

Compared to Trevor AI, Motion seems to be focused on executives and managers willing to pay a premium for advanced project management features. Trevor AI is the assistant for the everyday hero - by eliminating complexity and offsetting it with powerful AI automations, Trevor makes planning your day truly effortless.

How does Trevor AI compare to AkiFlow?

Compared to Trevor AI, AkiFlow’s main advantage seems to be the number of task integrations you can sync with. Trevor AI focuses on quality over quantity of integrations and provider powerful AI planning features on top of that solid foundation.

How does Trevor AI compare to Google Calendar or Microsoft Calendar?

Trevor AI doesn’t compare with popular calendar platforms, it integrates with them in real-time. You schedule a task - It pops up in Google Calendar. You move it in Google Calendar, it updates in Trevor.

Which tools does Trevor AI Integrate with?

Trevor AI integrates with both task managers and calendar platforms like: Google Calendar, Microsoft Calendar (Outlook & Office365), Apple Calendar, Google Tasks, Microsoft To-Do, and Todoist. For a complete integrations list visit our Integrations page.
