What is an AI Ad Generator?

An AI ad generator is a tool that turns your product details, text prompt, or campaign brief into ready-to-use video ads and static ads. Instead of designing from scratch, you enter a short input, and the AI generates layouts, headlines, videos, visuals, and ad copy you can edit and export.

How does an AI Ad Generator work?

It combines several AI techniques. Natural language processing reads your prompt. Generative AI models trained on large sets of ads produce the visuals and copy. Machine learning ranks and refines the variations. The result is a finished ad assembled from your input, which you can then customize.

What is generative AI, and how does it create ads?

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that produces images, video, and text rather than just analyzing existing data. In an AI ad generator, generative models trained on large datasets take your text prompt, product link, or image and generate original ad creatives, copy, and videos from it. Predis.ai combines several such models, including Google's Veo and Gemini and ByteDance's Seedance for video, with proprietary models tuned for advertising, so the output is built to convert, not just to look good.
