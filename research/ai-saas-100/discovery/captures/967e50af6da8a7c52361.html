Context graphTrail

Help agents solve the most complex problems with context graphs.

A customer send an email asking to return an order past the 30-day window, bought under an enterprise agreement, after three prior returns this quarter, and their rep promised an exception on a call. Do we accept it?

One single email requires five different kinds of context, each owned by a different team. The returns policy doesn’t know the call, the ERP doesn’t know the agreement, and so on. Our context graph, Trail, holds every rule cited to the document it came from.

How Trail, our context graph, works →

  • Returns policy

    Policy context ➔ What is allowed?

    30 days, so exceptions need a manager.

  • ERP order record

    System-of-record context ➔ What did they buy?

    SO-4471 is the enterprise agreement, dated 62 days ago.

  • Return history

    Computed context ➔ How often is this?

    There were 3 returns this quarter, $84K in total.

  • Call notes & email

    Conversation context ➔ What was promised?

    Rep agreed an exception on 4 Aug.

  • Exception SOP

    SOP context ➔ Who approves, in what order?

    Support lead → finance, logged on the order.

Claims Adjudication — Auto & Property

Confidential

Standard Operating Procedure

Purpose

Adjudicate each first-notice-of-loss claim against the active policy. Return a decision, the payable amount, and a rationale.

1. Coverage & eligibility

The deductible is applied before any payable amount is calculated.

2. Documentation requirements

  • Water damage — dated photos and a plumber's report.
  • Bodily injury — an itemized medical bill.

3. Escalation matrix
