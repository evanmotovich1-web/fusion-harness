How involved are the human experts — really?

Your strategist isn't just approving agent outputs — they're briefing agents, setting the strategic direction, editing content for brand voice and accuracy, and making the calls that require genuine expertise. Think of them as the senior teammate who uses AI the way a great surgeon uses the best tools: the human judgment is the point, not the equipment.

What AI engines do you optimize for beyond Google?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Grok. We track where your brand appears in AI answers across all five platforms, and our content is structured specifically to earn citations in those environments — not just to rank on Google.

How quickly will I see results?

Initial traction typically appears within 60–90 days. Google rankings compound from month 3–6. AI visibility can move faster — particularly when topical authority and structured content are strong from day one.

What's covered in the engagement?

Keyword and prompt strategy, SEO and AEO content creation, technical crawlability for Googlebot and AI crawlers, high-authority backlink building, Reddit activations, LLM mention tracking, and monthly strategy calls. Everything is managed — you don't need to touch a tool.

How is this different from hiring an SEO agency?

A traditional agency is bottlenecked by headcount. We're not. Our agents produce and optimize at a pace no human team can match, while your dedicated strategist ensures every output meets the quality bar of the best agency you've ever hired. You get both — without paying for both.
