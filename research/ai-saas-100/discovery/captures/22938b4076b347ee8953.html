DOCUMENT INTAKE

Your team spends more time chasing documents than processing them.

Inbound docs arrive across email, SFTP, drive folders, vendor portals, and scanned attachments. Half are missing pages. The latest version is buried in a reply-all thread. Your team becomes the routing layer copying attachments, classifying intent, asking "did you send the W-2?" for the third time.

Manual extraction

Your team is reading PDFs and typing the data into systems.

Every invoice, application, claim, and form means someone opens a document, reads the fields, and types them into NetSuite, Encompass, Epic, or your CRM. Slow. Expensive. Error-prone. The work scales linearly with volume - your team doesn't.

Case validation

Every case takes 20 minutes and a human to cross-check.

A loan packet, a claim, a KYC bundle. Each one needs someone to verify names match across three documents, calculate the DTI, confirm income against the W-2, and check the reserves. The work is rote, the volume is large, and one mistake costs the team a quarter.

Decision delivery

Decisions sit in a queue while customers wait.

By the time a document gets routed, extracted, validated, and reviewed, days have passed. Your SLA slipped. Your customer called twice. Your ops team is firefighting volume instead of improving the process.
