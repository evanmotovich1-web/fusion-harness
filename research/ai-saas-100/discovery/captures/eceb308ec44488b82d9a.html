You asked, we answer

What is AskYourPDF?

AskYourPDF is an AI platform that puts your content to work. Embed an AI agent on your website, auto-fill PDF forms from your own data, and chat with any document to pull out answers and summaries.

How do I add the AI agent to my website?

Point the agent at your site, let it train on your pages, add any documents, then paste a single line of code before the closing body tag. It works on plain HTML, WordPress, Webflow, Wix, Shopify, Squarespace, and frameworks like React, Vue, and Next.js.

What can I train the AI agent on?

Your website plus any files you upload, including PDF, TXT, PPT, PPTX, EPUB, and RTF, among others. It answers only from the sources you give it.

How does PDF form filling work?

Upload any fillable or non-fillable PDF form, pair it with data from another PDF, a text file, or pasted content, and AskYourPDF fills the fields automatically in seconds.

Can the agent use my documents and my website together?

Yes. You can add files alongside your website so the agent answers from both.

Can I use AskYourPDF on multiple platforms?

Yes. Alongside the web app, you can use the mobile apps, the Chrome extension, and plugins for Zotero and ChatGPT.

How do I chat with a document?

Upload your PDF or text document and start a chat to ask questions and pull key insights from the content.

Is my data secure?

Yes. AskYourPDF is GDPR compliant, with encryption and strict standards for data security and privacy.
