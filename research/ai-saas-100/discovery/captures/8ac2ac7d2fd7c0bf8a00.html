Frase publishes to your CMS.

Frase publishes to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, and Wix, or hosts the page on FraseCMS. Scheduled, formatted, schema attached. No copy and paste, no migration.

Every page gets a search score and an AI-readiness score, and Frase tracks your share of voice across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI every day. You know where you stand before a prospect tells you.

Common questions.

Do I have to move my site to Frase?

No. Frase publishes into the CMS you already use — WordPress, Webflow, Sanity or Wix. Or publish to FraseCMS: an agent-ready CMS, hosted or headless, on your own domain, with WebMCP so AI assistants can read your pages and act on them. Either way your content stays yours and exports whenever you want it.

Does Frase monitor whether AI cites me?

Yes. AI Visibility tracks where you show up across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI on a daily cadence, and alerts you when your share of voice moves. AI Visibility is a separate feature from Content Guard.

Does Frase fix pages automatically?

Content Guard watches your published pages for ranking and traffic decay in Google and drafts the fix. You approve each change. You can opt in to let Frase apply low-risk fixes automatically, but that stays your call.

How does Frase know my brand?

Two ways. Frase builds a brand profile from the content you already have, and you can set it yourself in the Brand Hub: your tone, your rules, the terms you use and the ones you never want used. Every draft is written against that, then checked against the live results you are competing with.

Is Frase for agencies?

Yes. On Scale, agencies get multi-site, multi-seat, exportable client-ready reports, and role-based access. White-label, branded client portals, and custom domains are available on Enterprise.

Does the free trial need a credit card?

No. The trial runs for 7 days with no credit card required. You add billing only when you choose a plan.

Can I run Frase from my own agent or terminal?

Bring your own agent. Frase runs where you already work — Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, your own terminal — through the MCP server and CLI, on every plan, so it researches, drafts and publishes without leaving the tool you are in. Point it at your site and let it run.

Do I need to be an SEO expert to use Frase?

No. Frase handles the research, drafts in your voice, and points to exactly what to improve before you publish. You bring the strategy and the judgment. Frase does the legwork.
