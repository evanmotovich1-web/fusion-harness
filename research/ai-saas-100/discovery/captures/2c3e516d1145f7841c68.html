Frequently asked questions

What is the Captions app?

Captions is an AI video editor that produces fully-edited videos. You can upload existing footage and use easy AI editing tools to finish the project. You can also use Captions to generate entirely new footage, without having to record on your own. Captions guides you through suggested edits with its AI editor, empowering you to make any video you want, regardless of expertise.

How do I get Captions?

You can use Captions on your phone or computer. To start online, sign up in your browser. Or, download the app from the App Store or Google Play.

Is Captions free to use?

Captions is free to download, and you can access some features without a subscription. To unlock all capabilities and get professional-quality videos, you’ll want to sign up for one of our paid plans.

What’s the best AI video editor for content creators?

The best AI video editor helps you create professional-quality content fast, without advanced editing skills—and that's exactly what Captions is built for. Unlike traditional editors, Captions uses AI to handle time-consuming tasks automatically, editing your entire video in just a couple of taps. Whether you're making short-form social videos, ads, tutorials, or promotional content, Captions delivers studio-quality results in minutes, making it a top choice for creators who want speed, quality, and accessibility all in one app.

See how Captions works for your niche.

What types of videos can Captions edit?

You can edit a wide range of video types and formats, including vertical and horizontal video. Captions works well for social content, video ads, podcast clips, tutorials, product assets, and more.

Does Captions edit my whole video for me?

Yes! Captions is designed to remove the heavy lifting so you can do the entire video all in one app. It handles everything from start to finish so you never have to edit by hand again. Once you upload or record your video, Captions automatically edits and stylizes your video with custom zooms, B-roll, music, transitions, and more. You can use our simple editor for any tweaks you want, but the time-consuming and specialized tasks are handled for you.

What happened to Mirage Studio?

We recently merged Mirage Studio with our other product, Captions. If you were a Mirage Studio subscriber, you can now access your account from Captions online. Once you log in, you'll see your existing projects and actors from your previous workspace. With this change, you will also gain access to new editing features within the Captions experience.
