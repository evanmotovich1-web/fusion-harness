FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What is Northlight?
Northlight is a native Mac app for building applets — living apps with AI workers inside them. It connects to the tools you already use and works across them, handling outreach, enrichment, research, and the busywork you'd rather not do.
What is a worker?
A worker is an AI teammate you set up once and leave running. It will ask you if it needs something and it will track its own KPIs for you to review.
How is this different from ChatGPT or Claude?
ChatGPT and Claude give you advice. Northlight takes action. It connects to your tools — LinkedIn, your CRM, email, and hundreds of other apps — and does the work across them, checking in with you before anything important.
Is this a browser extension?
No. Northlight is a native Mac app. It's straightforward to download and get started.
How do I get started?
Download the app, sign in, and start chatting. Northlight works through the accounts you're already logged into on your Mac and through direct connectors to your tools — so most things just work without handing over credentials.
Is my data safe?
Yes. We never store your passwords and we don't need your API keys. We're in process of getting SOC2 Type II.
Who is Northlight for?
Anyone can use Northlight. Right now our agent is particularly better for someone in a "relationship building" role and potentially way more. SDRs, BDRs, AEs, and revenue teams who want to automate repetitive tasks across their sales stack.
What can I actually do with it?
Prospect on LinkedIn, update your CRM, enrich contacts, run outreach sequences, process call transcripts, analyze your pipeline, and spin up small apps for one-off work — anything that spans the tools you already use.
Does it work on Windows?
Not yet. Northlight is currently macOS only. Windows support is on our roadmap.
How much does it cost?
Start free with 100 AI requests a month and full access to the product. Pro is $100/month for unlimited AI requests. See our pricing section for details.
