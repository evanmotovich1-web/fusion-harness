Automatic captions and translations

Storyboard-based video editing

For fine adjustments, Wisecut generates a storyboard transcribed from your speech. You edit by moving text and scenes — no keyframes, no complex timelines, no need to master editing software.

Automatically remove silences

Wisecut’s AI, together with auto-zoom, detects and cuts silent pauses, saving you time and keeping your videos flowing smoothly.

Smart background music

Wisecut picks a track and tunes the soundtrack to match your video. It also handles audio ducking: lowering the music while someone is speaking and raising it when there’s no speech.
